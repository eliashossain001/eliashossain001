### 👨‍🔬 About

I am a PhD candidate at the **University of Central Florida** (Complex Adaptive Systems Lab, advised by Dr. Niloofar Yousefi), with three years of industry ML experience before my PhD. Most deployed AI systems cannot be retrained by the people who rely on them, so my research asks what we can still guarantee at inference time:

- **Does the model know when it is wrong?** Calibrated uncertainty without retraining.
- **Is agreement among AI judges real evidence?** LLM judges share blind spots; I measure how much.
- **Can an agent be stopped before an unsafe action?** Runtime safety for tool-using and multi-agent systems.

My applied area is **clinical and biomedical AI**, where a confident error costs patient harm, not benchmark points.

### 🔬 Open-Source Code & Benchmarks

- **[NEXUS](https://github.com/eliashossain001/nexus)**: runtime safety monitor for tool-using LLM agents
- **[UAT-LITE](https://github.com/eliashossain001/uat-lite)**: inference-time uncertainty-aware attention, with released checkpoints
- **[PoE-Composition](https://github.com/eliashossain001/poe_composition)**: inference-time steering of frozen offline-RL policies
- **[Automatic-Jury-Consensus](https://github.com/eliashossain001/Automatic-Jury-Consensus)**: measuring error dependence among LLM judges
- **[Temporal Attractor Steering](https://github.com/eliashossain001/temporal-attractor-steering)**: parametric temporal conflict in open-weight LLMs
- **[MiniHealthLM](https://github.com/eliashossain001/MiniHealthLM)**: a ~600M-parameter transformer trained from scratch for healthcare text
- Public datasets on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/EliasHossain): `ptc-benchmark`, `BioDivergence-Silver-v1.0`, `nanobubbleeval`, `CancerAbstracts`
